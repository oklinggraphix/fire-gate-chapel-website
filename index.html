About us

A house of prayer, raising a fiery generation.

"The Fire Temple isn't just our motto — it's what we contend for every time we gather: the presence and fire of God resting on ordinary people."

Fire Gate Chapel Int'l Church is home to a warm, growing community in Dansoman, near Livingstone. We believe worship, the Word, and prayer are meant to change lives, not just fill a Sunday.

Under the leadership of Rev. Dr. Godwin Ahiahornu, we gather across the week for worship, teaching, and prayer — and we'd love for you to join us.
